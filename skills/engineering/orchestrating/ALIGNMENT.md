# Alignment gate without a confirmed record

Present one compact, numbered proposed read-back covering the intended outcome, in/out of scope, acceptance criteria, chosen design, and consequential assumptions or trade-offs. Use the issue and policy to ground it; verify facts yourself and ask targeted questions for gaps.

The operator's explicit confirmation of a complete, unchanged read-back satisfies the gate. If answers change it or fill a gap, give the revised read-back and ask for explicit confirmation. Invocation, silence, or an upstream brief with no recorded operator approval is not confirmation.

This is the last alignment check, not a replacement for grilling or grill-with-research. If no design is chosen or substantial uncertainty remains, stop setup and direct the operator back to grilling or grill-with-research, leaving the issue state, branch, and worktree unchanged. Reconcile any conflict with the authoritative issue specification before proceeding; the gate clarifies an existing design rather than selecting one or silently changing scope.

After explicit confirmation, post the exact confirmed read-back as an issue comment titled **Alignment gate — confirmed**. The operator's confirmation authorizes that comment.
